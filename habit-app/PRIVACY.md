# Daymark Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

Daymark is built so your habits stay yours.

- **Your habits stay on your device.** Everything you enter (habits, check-ins, reminders) is saved only on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iCloud or computer backups, which Apple and you control. We never receive it.
- **No accounts.** You never sign up or log in.
- **No advertising, analytics or tracking.** Daymark contains no ad, analytics or tracking code.
- **Purchases.** If you subscribe to Daymark Pro, Apple processes the payment. We use RevenueCat to confirm your subscription is active. RevenueCat receives an anonymous app user ID and your purchase history (no name, email or habit data). See RevenueCat's privacy policy at https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy.
- **Reminders and Streak Guard** notifications are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No server is involved.
- **Backup files** are created only when you tap "Export Backup", and go only where you choose to send them.

If you delete the app, its data is deleted from your device.
