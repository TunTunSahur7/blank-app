# Daymark Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 16, 2026_

Daymark does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

- **Your habits stay on your device.** Everything you enter is saved only on your iPhone. It is included in your normal iCloud or computer backups, which are controlled by Apple and by you.
- **No accounts.** You never sign up or log in.
- **No tracking or analytics.** Daymark contains no advertising, analytics, or tracking code, and it makes no network requests with your data.
- **Reminders** are scheduled locally on your iPhone. No server is involved.
- **Backup files** are created only when you tap "Export backup file", and go only where you choose to send them.

If you delete the app, its data is deleted from your device.
