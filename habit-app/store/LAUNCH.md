# Besa launch and growth plan

No one can guarantee a top-chart spot. What moves rankings is **download velocity in a short window**, **ratings**, and **retention**. This plan stacks all three.

## 1. Positioning (one sentence everywhere)

**"Besa: keep your word to yourself."** The name carries a story the press already knows: *besa* is the Albanian code of honor, keeping your word, famous worldwide for the Albanians who sheltered Jews in World War II. A habit is a promise to yourself; Besa helps you keep it. In product terms it is still **"the habit tracker that forgives you."** Unlimited habits free, rest days that don't break streaks, and a Streak Shield when life happens. Every competitor's 1-star reviews are about paywalled habit limits, lost streaks and surprise charges. Besa is the answer to each of them, so say it.

## 2. Money model (why it's built this way)

- **Free tier is generous on purpose.** Unlimited habits removes the #1 complaint and wins the reviews and word of mouth that drive ranking.
- **Pro sells loss aversion, not features.** Paywall moments sit where emotion is highest: a broken streak ("Save your 14-day streak"), locked history, Insights. Streak repair is one of the strongest proven monetization levers in habit and learning apps.
- **Yearly with a 7-day trial is the default plan.** It's where most subscription revenue comes from. Monthly is the anchor that makes yearly look cheap. **Lifetime** catches people with subscription fatigue (another top complaint).
- **Honest trial.** A trial timeline on the paywall, plus a reminder 2 days before billing. This lowers refunds and 1-star "scam" reviews, which protects ranking.
- **Rating prompts at moments of pride.** After 7+ day milestone celebrations, at most every 120 days.
- Run price tests later with RevenueCat Experiments ($29.99 vs $39.99 yearly) once you have ~1,000 paywall views.

## 3. Before launch (2–4 weeks out)

1. **Pre-order** in App Store Connect (up to 180 days ahead). Pre-orders all download on day one, creating a velocity spike.
2. **Apple featuring nomination:** App Store Connect › Featuring Nominations. Submit 3+ weeks before launch with the story: indie, privacy-first, fixes what users hate.
3. **Waitlist:** a one-page site with an email capture, and the 6 screenshots.
4. **Build in public** on X/Threads/TikTok: daily 15-second clips of the design and the "streak shield" idea.
5. Pitch indie-app roundups: TapSmart's monthly *Indie Apps Showcase*, MacStories, 9to5Mac, iMore.

## 4. Launch week (start on a Tuesday)

- **Product Hunt** the same day, with a maker comment telling the story. Ask the waitlist to visit (not to "upvote").
- **Reddit:** value-first posts in r/getdisciplined, r/productivity, r/iosapps, r/apple (App Saturday). Follow each sub's rules.
- **Hacker News:** "Show HN: A private, local-first habit tracker for iOS".
- **TikTok/Reels/Shorts:** post 3 videos a day for 10 days. Hooks that work in this category:
  - "I lost a 200-day streak because I got sick. So I built an app that forgives you."
  - "Every habit app limits you to 3 habits. Mine doesn't."
  - Screen recording of the 7-day celebration, with sound on.
  - Pack videos ride existing trends: "I tried Monk Mode for 30 days", "Dopamine detox day 1", "That-girl morning routine". Each one ends on adding the pack in one tap.
  - Widget aesthetic: a clean Home Screen setup with the Besa widget. Home Screen setup videos are their own genre.
- Reply to every review and every comment in the first two weeks.

## 5. After launch

- **Promotional text** can change anytime without review: rotate seasonal hooks ("New Year, new streak").
- **Seasonality:** January is the biggest month for habit apps by far. Spend your marketing there: ship an update and run Apple Search Ads in late December.
- **Apple Search Ads:** start with exact-match "habit tracker", "streaks", "habit app" at a capped daily budget. Watch cost per trial start, not cost per install.
- **Next features that sell Pro:** iCloud sync across devices, an Apple Watch app, and new habit packs timed to trends (a "New Year Reset" pack in late December).

## 6. Numbers to watch (RevenueCat dashboard)

- Paywall view → trial start rate
- Trial → paid conversion
- Day-1 / Day-7 retention
- Refund rate (keep it low: it hurts both ranking and revenue)
