# Sell fast phone accessories across the valley

**My pick is phone accessories, bought in Belgrade from Chinese wholesalers and Serbian distributors, then sold two ways: wholesale to the small phone shops and kiosks in Preševo, Bujanovac, Medveđa and Vranje, and retail from a market stall plus Instagram and Viber.** Of the five ideas, this is the only one that combines a high markup (often 200–400% on cheap items such as screen glass and cables, by our estimate) with weekly turnover, small and light stock, no customs, and demand from every age group. The honest answer to "can I double €5,000 in one month?" is: **probably not.** It is possible only in the best case, where you sell almost all your stock at good prices and reorder once. Our realistic base case is to end the month with about **€6,000 in cash plus about €1,400 of unsold stock**, a gain of roughly 30–50%. In a bad month you could lose €1,000–1,500 if you have to sell leftover stock cheaply. Most of the cost and margin numbers below are **our estimates, not published data**. Serbian wholesalers do not publish price lists, so you must check every price by phone or in person before you spend money. The legal setup matters too: **trade cannot use the cheap flat-rate tax (paušal) unless you sell from a kiosk or mobile structure**, every sale to a private person needs a fiscal receipt, and goods brought in from North Macedonia or Kosovo without customs papers can be seized.

## Phone accessories beat the other four ideas on markup and speed

The test for a one-month doubling is simple: you need a markup near 100% or more **and** stock that sells in days, not months. Only phone accessories pass both parts of the test. Fast lines such as tempered glass, cables and chargers are bought again and again. Retail prices in Serbia set a useful ceiling: phone cases sell for about **800–950 RSD (€7–8)** at Telefončić, and a single USB-C cable sells for **377 RSD (about €3.20)** at Tehnomanija ([Telefončić](https://telefoncic.rs/); [Tehnomanija](https://www.tehnomanija.rs/telefoni-smart-satovi-i-tableti/oprema-za-mobilne-telefone/punjaci-za-telefone-kablovi-i-adapteri)). Our estimate is that generic glass costs €0.20–0.50 and generic cases €0.50–1.50 at Chinese wholesale in Belgrade. That leaves room to sell to shops at about double your cost and to private buyers at three times your cost, while still being cheaper than the big chains.

The other four ideas each fail one part of the test. **Salon supplies:** branded professional products leave a reseller only about 20–40% between the distributor price and the salon price (our estimate). Getting to 100% means selling cheap unbranded Chinese or Turkish cosmetics. That is risky, because in Serbia the importer of cosmetics must be a registered legal entity that takes legal responsibility for the product, and sanitary inspectors can test samples at the importer's cost ([PKS cosmetics guide](https://api.pks.rs/storage/assets/Vodi%C4%8D%20za%20stavljanje%20kozmeti%C4%8Dkog%20proizvoda%20na%20tr%C5%BEi%C5%A1te.pdf); [spedicija-beograd.com](https://spedicija-beograd.com/blog/uvoz-kozmetike-u-srbiju/)). **Café packaging brokering** needs little money, because a logo cup costs about **9.90 RSD + VAT with a 1,000-piece minimum** ([Brand Pack](https://brandpack.rs/reklamna-casa-sa-logom/)). But the broker's share is only about 20–50%, and printers usually need 7–21 working days (both are estimates). You would complete one or two cycles in a month at most. **Car accessories:** branded car chemicals give about 30–60% markup, and sales peak in spring and just before winter (estimate). No wholesaler in the research openly sells to small car-care resellers. **Novi Pazar jeans and jackets** are the strongest runner-up, because November is the right season for winter jackets. However, the markup is about 50–100% (estimate) and stock turns over monthly, not weekly. The old "jeans valley" has also shrunk: the central market fell from about 600 to about 200 stalls, and local jeans now compete with cheap Chinese jeans ([RSE](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/da-li-je-novi-pazar-jos-uvek-dolina-dzinsa/26827870.html); [Vreme](https://vreme.com/vreme/sumrak-carstva-dzinsa/)). None of the Novi Pazar wholesalers we looked for had a website, so you would have to travel there just to see prices.

Phone accessories have real weaknesses too. Cases depend on the phone model: Telefončić stocks **more than 12,900 case models** ([Telefončić](https://telefoncic.rs/)). If you buy cases for the wrong phones, the money is stuck. Big chains such as Galaxy Code (20 shops in 12 cities) are also competitors ([PlanPlus](https://www.planplus.rs/galaxy-code-prateca-oprema-za-mobilne-telefone-i-tablete/86231)). The fix is to keep most of your money in products that fit every phone (glass sizes, cables, chargers, holders, earbuds) and to buy cases only for the 10–15 most common models you see in town.

## The local market is poor but compact, and December brings diaspora money

Preševo has **33,449 residents** (about 94% Albanian) and Bujanovac has **41,068**. With Medveđa (6,360), the valley has about 80,000 people ([Vreme](https://www.vreme.com/vesti/bez-bojkota-albanaca-duplo-vise-ljudi-u-presevu/); [citypopulation.de](https://citypopulation.de/en/serbia/admin/jablanica/M26146__medveđa/)). Incomes are the lowest in Serbia. The average net wage in July 2026 was **83,504 RSD (about €712)**, and only **3,923 people** had formal jobs in Q2 2025 ([Biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/prosecna-zarada-u-srbiji-u-julu-iznosila-121-346-dinara-medijalna-95-036-dinara/); [Danas/BBC Serbian](https://www.danas.rs/bbc-news-serbian/kako-se-zivi-u-presevu-i-bojniku-najsiromasnijim-opstinama-srbije/)). This works for phone accessories: they are cheap items that people buy even when money is short, because a cracked screen or a broken cable needs fixing now.

Diaspora money is the extra fuel. More than **70,000 people from the valley live abroad, more than 20,000 of them in Switzerland** ([Central Bank of Kosovo, citing *Le*](https://bqk-kos.org/governor-mehmeti-for-le-canton27-ch-the-kosovar-diaspora-last-year-brought-3-billion-euros-to-kosovo-switzerland-alone-over-600-million-chf/?lang=en)). In summer the streets fill with Swiss number plates ([Vreme](https://vreme.com/vreme/omorina-s-grmljavinom/)). November is the quiet season. A second, smaller wave of visitors probably arrives around Christmas and New Year, but this is our inference and is not documented. So your month will be slow at the start and better at the end. This is another reason to choose a product people need all year rather than a seasonal one.

Some important facts are still **unknown**. We found no count of phone shops, no rents, no market days and no data on where local shops buy their stock. Online directories hardly list Albanian-owned shops in Preševo. You must collect this information yourself in the first days (see the plan below).

## Unit economics show doubling needs near-total sell-through

All costs below are **estimates** to be checked. Prices are in euros at about 117 RSD per euro.

| Item | Your landed cost (est.) | Price to shops (~2x) | Retail price (~3x, below chains) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Tempered glass | €0.30–0.50 | €0.80–1.00 | €3–5 |
| Generic case, top models | €0.70–1.50 | €2–3 | €5–7 (chains: €7–8) |
| USB-C / Lightning cable | €0.60–1.00 | €1.50–2.00 | €2.50–3.50 (chain: €3.20) |
| Wall charger (with papers) | €2–3 | €4–5 | €7–10 |
| Car phone holder, earbuds | €1.50–4 | €3–7 | €6–12 |

Suggested budget for €5,000: about **€3,500 for first stock**, about **€500 for setup and running costs**, and **€1,000 kept as a reserve** for one restock trip in week 3. The €500 for costs is also an estimate. It covers registration, an accountant, the fiscal app, two Belgrade trips (about 350 km each way), a market table, printed price cards and small social-media ads.

| Scenario | What happens | End of month (cash + unsold stock at cost) | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| Best case | Restock with the €1,000 reserve; sell 90% of €4,500 stock, 60% of it at retail | about €10,600 | roughly doubled |
| Base case | Sell 60% of €3,500 stock, half retail, half to shops | about €6,000 cash + €1,400 stock = €7,400 | +30% to +50% |
| Bad case | Sell 25%, mostly to shops at low prices; dump the rest at half price | about €3,700–4,000 | loss of €1,000–1,300 |

The best case shows how hard doubling really is. It needs about **€7,300 of retail sales in 30 days, roughly 50 small items every day**, plus about €2,900 of sales to shops. That is possible only if you get many shops ordering every week and a busy market or street spot. Tax also comes out of profit. As a bookkeeping entrepreneur you pay 10% tax on real profit plus minimum social contributions. The exact monthly minimum for 2026 was not found, so ask an accountant.

## Register first, issue fiscal receipts, buy only inside Serbia

**You must register before you sell.** Buying goods to resell them regularly is trade, and there is no small-seller exception for resale in Serbia ([mojnovisad.com](https://www.mojnovisad.com/novi-zakoni-menjaju-onlajn-prodaju-inspekcija-proverava-i-gradane-na-kp-ananasu-limundu/)). Inspectors watch Instagram and Facebook sellers, work with the postal service to find them, and seize goods ([Telegraf](https://www.telegraf.rs/vesti/ekonomija/3026293-inspektori-jure-nelegalne-trgovce-na-fejsbuku-i-instagramu-a-onda-im-upadaju-u-kucu-na-udaru-su-majstori-i-prodavci-stvari-koje-najcesce-narucujemo)). Fines for unregistered activity are cited at **50,000–500,000 RSD** ([RTV](https://www.rtv.rs/sr_lat/vojvodina/novi-sad/visoke-kazne-za-neregistrovane-preduzetnike_687849.html)). Registration as a preduzetnik (sole trader) is online only, through the APR portal. You need a qualified electronic certificate and a payment card ([N1](https://n1info.rs/biznis/apr-obavezna-e-registracija-visestruko-uvecala-broj-firmi-osnovanih-elektronski/)). Get the certificate before November starts, because it can take a few days.

**Tax form:** retail and wholesale trade are **excluded from paušal**. The exception is trade from a **kiosk, trailer or similar temporary or mobile structure**, which can ask for flat-rate tax ([pausalko.rs](https://pausalko.rs/blog/sifre-delatnosti-pausalci-ko-moze-da-bude-pausalac/); [beoecc.rs, ZPDG Art. 40](https://www.beoecc.rs/clanovi-40-42-zakona-o-porezu-na-dohodak-gradana/)). For this plan, register as a **bookkeeping entrepreneur (knjigaš)** and hire a local accountant. Consider the kiosk route only if you find a cheap kiosk with a municipal permit, and confirm it with the accountant first. The deadline to request paušal for 2027 is **2 November 2026**; a new business asks for it at registration ([hitradio.rs](https://hitradio.rs/preduzetnici-imaju-rok-do-2-novembra-za-prijavu-pausalnog-oporezivanja)).

**Fiscal receipts:** every sale to a private person needs a fiscal receipt. Trade is not exempt, and fines are **50,000–500,000 RSD**. The cheapest setup is the **free "ESIR Poreska uprava RS" Android app** with the free security element from the Tax Administration ([pausalko.rs](https://pausalko.rs/blog/da-li-pausalci-moraju-da-imaju-fiskalnu-kasu-i-ko-je-oslobodjen-obaveze-evidentiranja-prometa-preko-kase/); [epreduzetnik.rs](https://epreduzetnik.rs/esir-aplikacija-poreske-uprave/)). Sales to other shops need an invoice, not a fiscal receipt. VAT-registered buyers will often want the invoice through the state e-invoice system SEF; the exact SEF duties for small non-VAT sellers are not fully clear, so check with your accountant ([biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/kako-e-fakture-mogu-slati-i-evidentirati-pausalci/)). Market-stall sellers are exempt from fiscal receipts **only until 31 December 2026**, and they must still be registered. Do not build your business on that exemption.

**Customs:** buy in Belgrade with a Serbian invoice and there is no customs at all. Do not bring stock from Skopje, Kumanovo or Gjilan in your car. The €100 traveller allowance is for personal use only. Goods for resale need a customs declaration (JCI) through a customs agent, and you pay **20% VAT on value plus duty**, which as a non-VAT business you cannot get back ([biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/preduzetnik/porezi/sta-se-sve-moze-uneti-u-srbiju-bez-carine/); [fakturko.io](https://fakturko.io/blog/uvoz-robe-kako-izracunati-pdv-i-carinu)). Online orders from Temu or AliExpress always pay VAT, and above €50 the total charge is about **32%** plus a 200 RSD fee per parcel ([Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/dnevne-vesti/170326/170326-vest8.html)). Use them only for samples. **Do not sell fake brand items** ("Apple", "Samsung" logos on copies), and for chargers ask the seller for a declaration of conformity. Without papers, an inspector can seize them.

**Optional extra money:** the state employment service (NSZ) gives **380,000 RSD (about €3,250)** to unemployed people who start a business ([biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/otvoren-poziv-za-dodelu-subvencija-za-samozaposljavanje-u-2026-godini/)). You must be registered as unemployed **before** you register the business, and most 2026 calls are closed. Some calls exclude trade. Ask the NSZ office before registering, because registering first can make you lose this chance.

## A 30-day plan with named suppliers and clear stop signals

**Suppliers to contact (call before you travel; ask for prices, a PIB invoice, and whether you can reorder by Viber):** **Eurasia Trade Center** in New Belgrade, the large Chinese wholesale centre that opened in September 2024 with up to about 460 shops ([Biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/proradio-novi-kineski-trzni-centar-u-beogradu/); [Kurir](https://biznis.kurir.rs/info-biz/9447584/otvaranje-novog-kineskog-trznog-centra)); **Condor Mobile**, a wholesaler of phones and accessories at Južni bulevar 124, Belgrade, tel. 011/4141-741 ([PlanPlus](https://www.planplus.rs/beograd/mobilni-telefoni)); **Ms Mobile**, accessories and spare parts wholesale at Bulevar vojvode Mišića 14, Belgrade, tel. 011/6556-777 ([PlanPlus](https://www.planplus.rs/ms-mobile-veleprodaja-mobilne-opreme-i-rezervnih-delova/101499)); **Nevski d.o.o.**, wholesale cases for Apple and Samsung ([nevski.rs](https://www.nevski.rs/veleprodaja-maski-za-telefon.html)); and **Velteh Pro**, a Belgrade importer and wholesaler of audio, video and car equipment, useful for car holders and chargers ([PlanPlus](https://www.planplus.rs/beograd/mobilni-telefoni)). Use Telefončić and Tehnomanija retail prices as your price ceiling.

| Days | What to do | Goal |
|---|---|---|
| Before 1 Nov | Get the e-certificate. Walk the main streets of Preševo and Bujanovac. Count phone shops and kiosks. Ask 10–15 owners where they buy, what runs out, and what they pay. Note the 15 most common phone models. Ask NSZ about the subsidy. | Shop list, price list, model list |
| 1–4 | Register as a preduzetnik (knjigaš) on APR. Hire an accountant. Install the ESIR app. Phone the suppliers for prices. | Legal to sell |
| 5–7 | First Belgrade trip. Spend about €3,500: 60% universal items (glass, cables, chargers, holders, earbuds), 40% cases for top models only. Get invoices and charger papers. | Stock in hand |
| 8–14 | Visit every phone shop, kiosk and repair shop in Preševo, Bujanovac, Medveđa and Vranje with samples and a simple Albanian/Serbian price list. Offer weekly delivery and Viber orders. Start a market table and an Instagram page with your business name and PIB. Issue a fiscal receipt for every retail sale. | First shop orders; daily retail |
| 15–21 | Check what sold. Use the €1,000 reserve to restock **only** the best sellers, by Viber order or a short trip. | No money in slow items |
| 22–30 | Second round of shop deliveries. Push gift bundles (case + glass + cable) for December diaspora visitors. Count cash and stock on day 30. | Decide: grow, hold or stop |

**Kill criteria (stop or change course if any is true):** before buying, if Belgrade landed costs are more than half of what local shops already pay, the margin is not there, so do not buy. By **day 10**, if fewer than **5 shops** have ordered and retail is under **€50 per day**, stop buying cases and keep only universal items. By **day 14**, if less than **20% of stock** has sold, do not restock; sell the remainder to shops at cost plus 10% and protect your cash. At any time, if an inspector questions a product line (chargers, logos), drop that line at once. On **day 30**, if total sales are below about **€3,000**, the business is a side income, not a doubling machine, so decide calmly whether to continue at a small scale.

## Conclusion

The real lesson is that no idea on this list reliably doubles €5,000 in 30 days, and anyone who promises that is guessing. Phone accessories are the best bet because they are the only option where the doubling path is at least visible: cheap goods, high markup, weekly reorders, no customs, and a buyer in every household. The biggest uncertainties are local and cheap to check: how many shops there are, what they pay now, and whether they will buy from a new local supplier. Spend the last days of October answering those questions before you spend a single euro on stock.

The more valuable result of this month may be what comes after it. If ten or more shops in the valley start ordering from you every week, you have built a small Albanian-speaking distribution route that most Belgrade wholesalers do not serve. You can later add salon disposables or café cups to the same delivery round, at low extra cost, and you will be in place before the summer diaspora season, when spending in Preševo is highest.
