# Suppliers, wholesale vs retail prices, and gross margins in Serbia: five candidate categories for a €5,000 reseller in Preševo

Research date: 27 Oct 2026. About 20 search and fetch calls were made. Search coverage of Serbian B2B price lists was **poor**: most wholesalers do not publish price lists and want a PIB/company account. Three supplier sites (brandpack.rs, lunipa.rs, europaksistem.rs) could not be fetched (DNS failure from the research environment), so their figures below come from search snippets only. Anything marked **[ESTIMATE]** is the researcher's judgement and has no source. The report writer should present it as such.

---

## Q1. Which categories realistically allow ~100% markup AND fast (weekly) turnover?

### Takeaway
No source gives margins or turnover for these categories in Serbia, so the ranking below is inference. Phone accessories and salon consumables are most likely to combine a 100%+ markup with weekly reorders. Printed packaging offers fast B2B repeat orders but low per-unit margin and needs upfront MOQs. Novi Pazar textiles have decent markup but are seasonal and slow. Car-care has moderate margins and slower turnover.

### Cited Findings
- Retail phone cases in Serbia sell at about 800–950 RSD (Telefončić), and a single USB-C cable retails at 377 RSD (Tehnomanija). — [Telefončić](https://telefoncic.rs/); [Tehnomanija](https://www.tehnomanija.rs/telefoni-smart-satovi-i-tableti/oprema-za-mobilne-telefone/punjaci-za-telefone-kablovi-i-adapteri)
- Telefončić advertises over 12,900 case models and 1,100 screen protectors. This shows how much SKU variety the category needs: a case is model-specific, so a small reseller carries dead-stock risk. — [Telefončić](https://telefoncic.rs/)
- Professional gel polish sells at about 1,400 RSD per bottle (Roniki Gel lak CM01) and No-Wipe Top Coat at 1,490 RSD. The site does not say whether these are retail or salon prices. — [Roniki Srbija](https://www.roniki.rs/)
- Salon service price for gel polish is 2,200–2,700 RSD (examples from Belgrade and Novi Sad). SrediMe lists "from 300–1,000 RSD" minimums in different areas. Salons buy consumables continuously, so demand recurs. — [SrediMe Novi Sad](https://www.sredime.rs/novisad/saloni-pedikir-manikir/gel-lak); [SrediMe Beograd](https://www.sredime.rs/beograd/saloni-pedikir-manikir/gel-lak)
- A logo-printed paper cup is about 9.90 RSD + VAT, with a 1,000-piece MOQ (Brand Pack, search snippet). — [Brand Pack](https://brandpack.rs/reklamna-casa-sa-logom/)

### Inferences
- **[ESTIMATE] Phone accessories:** generic Chinese cases, cables, tempered glass and chargers typically cost €0.30–2 landed from Chinese wholesalers or Belgrade Chinese centres. They retail at 500–1,500 RSD (€4–13), so markup is often 200–400% on cases and glass. Fast-moving lines (tempered glass, cables, chargers) turn over weekly. Model-specific cases turn over slowly.
- **[ESTIMATE] Salon consumables (B2B to salons):** a reseller sits between the distributor and the salon. Branded professional products give a distributor-to-reseller spread of only about 20–40%. To reach ~100% markup a small reseller would need unbranded or Chinese/Turkish private-label gel polish, lashes, gloves and foils, which carries quality and regulatory risk (cosmetics must comply with Serbian cosmetic product rules). Gloves, foils, wax and developer are reordered weekly by active salons.
- **[ESTIMATE] Printed packaging:** markup on the print job is about 20–50%. Volume and repeat orders from cafés are the appeal, but each client's order is custom, so no stock turnover is involved. It works better as a brokered service (take a deposit, then order from the printer) than as stock.
- **[ESTIMATE] Car accessories/care:** air fresheners and microfibre cloths can reach 100%+ markup. Branded chemicals (Sonax, Areon) are more like 30–60%. Turnover is moderate and peaks in spring and before winter (antifreeze, scrapers, wipers).
- **[ESTIMATE] Novi Pazar textiles/jeans/jackets:** markup is 50–100% at retail. Stock is seasonal: winter jackets sell Oct–Jan and dowry sets peak in the summer wedding season. Turnover is monthly, not weekly.

### Gaps
- No published Serbian data on gross margins or stock turnover for any of the five categories was found. All margin and turnover figures above are estimates.

## Q4. Cheaper sourcing in Skopje, Kosovo, Turkey, or online (Alibaba/Temu): is it practical?

### Takeaway
Small online orders come into Serbia duty-free up to €50 but still pay 20% VAT. Above €50 the total levy is about 32%. Commercial imports need a registered firm and customs clearance. Kosovo lifted its ban on Serbian goods, but the flow relevant here is the reverse, and cross-border commercial carrying needs customs formalities. No sources were found on Skopje or Gjilan wholesale for Serbian buyers.

### Cited Findings
- Parcels from companies (Temu, AliExpress) are duty-free up to €50, but **VAT is always due**. Gifts from private persons are duty-free up to €70. — [N1](https://n1info.rs/biznis/ako-kupujete-preko-stranih-aplikacija-kada-se-placa-carina-a-kada-pdv/); [Paragraf](https://www.paragraf.rs/dnevne-vesti/040924/040924-vest10.html)
- **2026 Pošta Srbije rules:**
  - a customs processing fee of 200 RSD per parcel
  - parcels up to €50: 20% VAT plus a 60 RSD commission
  - parcels over €50: 10% duty plus VAT, about 32% in total

  Sources conflict on whether Temu prepays VAT at checkout. — [Paragraf 2026](https://www.paragraf.rs/dnevne-vesti/170326/170326-vest8.html); [Ozon](https://www.ozon.rs/vesti/2026/nova-naplata-za-pakete-iz-inostranstva-evo-koliko-ce-gradjani-sada-placati-posti-srbije/); [trenutno.rs](https://www.trenutno.rs/carina-na-temu-i-aliexpress-pakete-srbija/)
- Kosovo lifted its June 2023 ban on Serbian goods. Serbia's exports to Kosovo then exceeded Albania's (€10.3M vs €7.1M in January). In 2025 Kosovo imported €234.8M from Serbia and exported €61.5M to it. Kosovo later added new measures, including an aluminium quota and a temporary livestock/pork ban. — [Kosovo Online](https://www.kosovo-online.com/en/news/analysis/five-months-after-import-ban-lifted-serbian-goods-increasingly-demand-kosovo-1-3-2025); [KOHA](https://www.koha.net/en/lajmet-e-mbremjes-ktv/kosova-vendos-masa-te-reja-tregtare-ndaj-serbise); [EuroMeatNews](https://euromeatnews.com/kosovo-temporarily-bans-imports-of-live-animals-and-pork-from-serbia/)
- Skopje's Bit Pazar is mainly a produce market with some clothing, electronics and Chinese goods. No source describes it as a wholesale hub for Serbian buyers. — [In Your Pocket](https://inyourpocket.com/north-macedonia/skopje/venues/bit-pazar)

### Inferences
- [ESTIMATE] Temu or AliExpress is practical only for testing samples, given the €50 and 32% thresholds and the per-parcel fees. For stock worth €1,000+, a single Alibaba consolidated shipment or a buying trip to the Belgrade Chinese centres is likely cheaper once duty is counted. Cases, cables and chargers attract EU-style duties (roughly 0–14%) plus 20% VAT.
- [ESTIMATE] Turkey (Istanbul Laleli/Merter for textiles, Osmanbey for clothing) is a common sourcing route for Balkan clothing traders. It requires a registered importer, cargo forwarding and customs, which is high overhead for €5,000.
- [ESTIMATE] Carrying goods for resale from Kosovo or North Macedonia into Serbia without declaring them is illegal, and the Preševo area has customs crossings nearby. The report should not recommend this route.

### Gaps
- No sources were found on wholesale price levels in Skopje, Pristina or Gjilan versus Serbia, or on Serbian buyers sourcing there.
- No Turkish wholesale price data was retrieved.
- Exact Serbian customs duty rates by HS code for phone accessories, cosmetics and textiles were not retrieved.
