# Local Market of Preševo (Preshevë) and the Preševo Valley: customers, business segments, supply chains

Research date: 27 October 2026. Method note: many primary pages (danas.rs, 021.rs, vreme.com, bqk-kos.org, kt.gov.rs, citypopulation.de) could not be fetched directly because of DNS failures in the research environment. Facts marked "(via search snippet)" come from search-engine extracts of those pages, not a full read, so they should be spot-checked. Coverage of business density, rents, market days and supplier habits is thin online. Most of it is local knowledge that would need on-the-ground verification, such as walking the main streets, asking traders, or checking local Facebook groups.

---

## 1. Customers: population, ethnicity, age, jobs, wages, diaspora and seasonality

### Takeaway
Preševo municipality has about 33,400 residents, roughly 94% of them Albanian (2022 census). It has the lowest average net wage in Serbia: about 72,000–84,000 RSD a month, or €615–715, in 2025–2026. Formal employment is very low, with about 3,900 employed people in Q2 2025. Local spending therefore leans heavily on a large diaspora, mainly in Switzerland and Germany. That diaspora is very visible in summer: streets "full of Swiss plates". Bujanovac (41,068 residents, about 62% Albanian) and Medveđa (6,360) complete the valley's market, which totals roughly 80,000 people.

**Employment and unemployment**
- RZS counted only 3,923 employed people in Preševo in Q2 2025, in a municipality of more than 33,000 residents. — [Danas/BBC Serbian](https://www.danas.rs/bbc-news-serbian/kako-se-zivi-u-presevu-i-bojniku-najsiromasnijim-opstinama-srbije/) (via search snippet)

- Emigrant associations from Preševo, Bujanovac and Medveđa in Switzerland organised a joint "historic assembly" in 2021 to coordinate support for the home region. — [Kosovo Online, 31 Oct 2021](https://www.kosovo-online.com/vesti/politika/u-svajcarskoj-sabor-albanaca-iz-preseva-bujanovca-i-medvedje-31-10-2021)

**Seasonality of diaspora returns**
- On a 35°C summer day the streets of Preševo were full of people and cars, "dominated by those with Swiss registration plates", with the local market as a focal point. — [Vreme, "Omorina s grmljavinom"](https://vreme.com/vreme/omorina-s-grmljavinom/) (via search snippet; article date not confirmed)
- The current Preševo mayor (Arifi, himself from the diaspora) says his family visited Preševo every year and came back for the summer holidays. — [Telegrafi](https://telegrafi.com/arifi-ti-sjellim-investimet-e-huaja-ne-presheve-paraardhesit-e-mi-e-shkaterruan-kete-komune/)
- In neighbouring Kosovo, about 400,000 diaspora members come home each summer, and some save all year to spend during the holiday. This shows the regional pattern. — [Telegrafi, "Mërgimtarët – vijnë, shpenzojnë dhe kthehen"](https://telegrafi.com/mergimtaret-vijne-shpenzojne-dhe-kthehen/)

### Inferences
- The valley's resident market is about 80,000 people (33.4k + 41.1k + 6.4k). The Albanian-speaking core is about 57,000 people in Preševo and Bujanovac. Preševo town is the commercial hub for Albanian-speaking customers.
- Purchasing power is two-tier. Residents earn about €620–710 a month when employed, and few are employed. Diaspora households bring Swiss or German income, which peaks from about mid-July to the end of August. A probable second, smaller peak falls around late December and New Year, plus Bajram/Eid holidays. A November 2026 launch would therefore open in the low season, about two months before the winter diaspora wave and eight months before the main summer wave.
- Summer is wedding season in diaspora-heavy Albanian communities. That drives demand for beauty services, dowry/textiles (pajë), gifts and clothing. I found no Preševo-specific source confirming this, so treat it as an inference.

### Gaps
- No 2022 census age breakdown for Preševo municipality was retrieved; it should be in the RZS 2022 census books (stat.gov.rs).
- No official NSZ count of registered unemployed in Preševo for 2025–2026 was found.
- No verified figure for remittances to the valley. The €100m+ estimate is unsourced. The National Bank of Serbia does not publish municipal remittance data.
- No quantified data on diaspora visit timing or spending specific to Preševo.

## 2. Local business segments: counts and density

### Takeaway
I found no current, citable count of beauty or nail salons, barbers, cafés, phone or repair shops, car washes, auto parts shops or clothing/dowry shops in Preševo. The online directories (PlanPlus, 11888) list almost nothing for the town. The only structured count is old and undated: 80 legal entities and 214 sole-trader businesses (radnje) in Preševo, against 135 + 244 in Bujanovac and 3 + 60 in Medveđa. Segment density has to be established by a physical or Google Maps/Facebook count.

### Cited Findings
- From a South Serbia economic strategy (undated, probably 2000s; treat as outdated): Preševo had 80 legal entities and 214 sole-trader shops (294 in total). Bujanovac had 135 and 244 (379). Medveđa had 3 and 60 (63). The three together had 736. — [Strategija dugoročnog ekonomskog razvoja juga Srbije (Otvorena vlada)](https://otvorenavlada.rs/strategija-jug-srbije177a-lat-doc-2/) (via search snippet)
- Sole traders (preduzetnici) make up a somewhat larger share of businesses in Bujanovac and Preševo than the Serbian average, and companies a smaller share. — [European PROGRES study on women in the labour market, Bujanovac and Preševo](http://www.europeanprogres.org/dokumenti/48_908148_studija-srb-new.pdf) (via search snippet; date not confirmed)
- Nationally, APR counted 414,705 business entities in early 2022: 124,260 companies and 290,445 sole traders. — [APR](https://www.apr.gov.rs/vesti.2428.html?newsId=3039) (via search snippet)
- PlanPlus has essentially no listings for hair or beauty salons in Preševo. The only Preševo entries returned were for the Auto-Moto Savez (AMSS) at the Miratovac border crossing and Čukarka. — [PlanPlus AMSS Preševo](https://www.planplus.rs/auto-moto-savez-presevo/63304)
- RZS publishes "Enterprises by size and entrepreneurs in Serbia 2021–2023", which may contain municipal breakdowns (not retrieved). — [RZS, 24 Jul 2025](https://www.stat.gov.rs/sr-latn/vesti/20250724-preduzeca-po-velicini-i-preduzetnici-u-republici-srbiji-2021-2023/)
- Preševo municipality has a local economic development strategy document. — [Izrada lokalno ekonomske strategije opštine Preševo (kt.gov.rs)](https://kt.gov.rs/images/sp_presevo_sr.pdf) (could not be fetched)
- Business-community activity: the Business Union "Vision" and the Global Network of Albanian Businesses held the 3rd Economic Conference "The Economy Saves the Valley", covering agriculture, product certification and market access. — [Gazeta Tema / search result context](https://english.gazetatema.net/kosovo-region/albanian-mp-in-serbia-seeks-10m-for-preevo-valley-i341504) (via search snippet; exact source page uncertain)

### Inferences
- Because online directories underrepresent Albanian-owned businesses in Preševo (which mostly market through Facebook and Instagram in Albanian), PlanPlus and 11888 counts would badly undercount. The most reliable approach is a Google Maps category sweep plus a street walk-through.
- The large share of sole traders points to a market of many very small shops and service points, which fits a €5,000 entry.

### Gaps
- No counts were found for any segment the brief asks about (salons, barbers, cafés/fast food, phone shops/repair, car washes/auto parts, clothing/dowry). This is the most important item to verify on the ground.
- Current APR totals of active entities by municipality for Preševo, Bujanovac and Medveđa were not retrieved.
- Local outlets named in the brief (Presheva Jonë, Titulli) did not appear in search results.

## 3. Supply sources, cross-border shopping and price differences

### Takeaway
I found no direct documentation of where Preševo shops buy their stock. The structural facts are these. Preševo sits on Corridor X next to the Preševo–Tabanovce crossing into North Macedonia (Kumanovo and Skopje are close), which has run as a joint one-stop crossing since 2019–2021. Kosovo (Gjilan) is reached through administrative crossings such as Končulj. Supply from Kosovo has been disrupted: Kosovo banned Serbian goods from June 2023, cutting bilateral trade by about half, with only partial easing later. That makes Kosovo-sourced trade awkward in both directions.

### Cited Findings
- The integrated Preševo–Tabanovce crossing, with Serbian and North Macedonian customs and police in one facility, started operating in 2019 and was expected to shorten queues and boost trade. — [Serbian Government](https://www.srbija.gov.rs/vest/en/144300/integrated-presevo-tabanovce-border-crossing-starts-operating.php); [European Western Balkans, Aug 2019](https://europeanwesternbalkans.com/2019/08/27/serbia-and-north-macedonia-open-integrated-border-crossing/)
- A recent waiting-time snapshot at Preševo/Tabanovce showed about 10 minutes. — [BorderAlarm](https://borderalarm.com/bottlenecks/presevo-tabanovce/)
- An expansion of the Preševo border crossing to 23 lanes is planned. — [Biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/u-planu-prosirenje-granicnog-prelaza-presevo/)
- Končulj is an administrative crossing between central Serbia and Kosovo. — [Wikipedia: Končulj](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Kon%C4%8Dulj)
- Kosovo banned Serbian goods after June 2023 and later amended the decision to cover only goods originating in Serbia. — [N1](https://n1info.rs/english/news/kosovo-amends-decision-bans-only-serbian-goods/)
- According to the head of the Kosovo Business Alliance, Kosovo–Serbia trade fell from more than €443m in 2022 to about €256m the next year. Traders who imported only from Serbia were hit hardest, some companies closed, and goods are now sourced from further away, which raises prices. — [Kosovo Online](https://www.kosovo-online.com/en/news/politics/sahini-due-ban-imports-serbia-greatest-harm-serbian-citizens-and-albanians-were-also); [Kosovo Online, Jun 2024](https://www.kosovo-online.com/en/news/analysis/ban-serbian-goods-kosovo-has-been-effect-full-year-eu-standing-idly-14-6-2024)
- Kosovo later reopened imports at one crossing, 16 months after the ban. — [ABC News/AP](https://abcnews.go.com/International/wireStory/kosovo-lifts-ban-entry-products-serbia-border-114562354)
- Politicians have periodically revived the idea of a Preševo–Gjilan–Kumanovo micro-region. — [KoSSev](https://kossev.info/en/?p=45468) (via search snippet)

### Inferences
- The likely supply hubs for a small Preševo trader are:
  - Skopje and Kumanovo (North Macedonia): about 15–50 km, Albanian-speaking wholesalers, and generally lower prices on many consumer goods. Imports into Serbia are subject to Serbian customs and VAT; CEFTA origin rules apply.
  - Niš and Belgrade: no customs, VAT invoices that support legal registration in Serbia, and access to official distributors (for example, salon cosmetics brands).
  - Gjilan and Pristina: language and family links, but complicated by the trade tensions above.

  These are inferences from geography and the cited trade facts, not documented local practice.
- For a registered Serbian business, buying from Serbian distributors (Niš, Belgrade) avoids customs paperwork. Cross-border sourcing from North Macedonia requires import formalities; informal "suitcase" imports carry legal risk.

### Gaps
- No source documents actual supplier habits of Preševo salons, phone shops or clothing shops, or cross-border consumer shopping (for example, residents buying in Kumanovo or Skopje).
- No price comparisons between Preševo and North Macedonia or Kosovo were found. A manual check of a basket of products is needed (salon consumables, phone accessories, textiles).

## 4. Market days, main commercial streets, retail rents

### Takeaway
I found no online source for Preševo's market days, the names of its commercial streets or its retail rents. The only cited detail is that the town's market is a summer hub crowded with diaspora cars. Rents must be collected locally (Facebook groups, local agents, direct inquiry). Belgrade benchmarks of €15–40/m² are irrelevant and far above what Preševo would charge.

### Cited Findings
- Preševo's local market (pijaca) is a focal point of town life in summer, when the streets are dominated by cars with Swiss plates. — [Vreme, "Omorina s grmljavinom"](https://vreme.com/vreme/omorina-s-grmljavinom/) (via search snippet; day of the week not given)
- The municipality publishes spatial and detailed regulation plans that may identify the central commercial zones: the Spatial Plan of Preševo (April 2026) and a detailed regulation plan (February 2025). — [PPO Preševo (presevo.rs, 2026)](https://presevo.rs/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/tekst_PPO-Presevo_preciscen-1.pdf); [PDR (presevo.rs, Feb 2025)](https://presevo.rs/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/PDR-SE-Presevo-1_RJU_februar-2025.pdf) (not fetched)
- For reference only, Belgrade commercial rents run about €15–40/m² (2023). — [Biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/novac/investicije/iznajmljivanje-lokala-u-beogradu-i-do-40-evra-po-kvadratu/)

### Inferences
- Given wage levels at about 60–70% of Serbia's average and low formal economic activity, small-shop rents in Preševo centre are likely a small fraction of Belgrade levels. Kiosks or small shops are probably in the low hundreds of euros a month at most. This is an estimate with no source and must be verified.

### Gaps
- Market day or days in Preševo are unconfirmed. Searches in Serbian ("pijaca", "pazarni dan") and Albanian ("pazari", "tregu") returned nothing.
- Main commercial street names are unconfirmed. They could be checked against the PDR or PPO plan documents above or on a map.
- No listings or rent levels were found on Halo Oglasi or other portals for Preševo, Bujanovac or Medveđa.

## 5. Evidence of unmet demand or gaps

### Takeaway
I found no direct evidence of specific unmet demand, such as a missing local distributor of salon supplies or packaging. Indirect signals: the valley is under-served by formal distribution (very few legal entities, mostly sole traders), it is underinvested (the "€10m fund" appeal), and its Albanian-speaking customers are poorly served by Serbian-language directories and distributors. A small Albanian-speaking B2B supplier based locally (salon or barber consumables, café or fast-food disposables and packaging, phone accessories) is a plausible niche, but it is unproven.

### Cited Findings
- The valley has had about 25 years of underinvestment and needs funds for jobs, farming and infrastructure. — [Gazeta Tema](https://english.gazetatema.net/kosovo-region/albanian-mp-in-serbia-seeks-10m-for-preevo-valley-i341504)
- The business base is small and dominated by sole traders (294 entities in Preševo in the old count; companies under-represented relative to Serbia). — [Otvorena vlada strategy](https://otvorenavlada.rs/strategija-jug-srbije177a-lat-doc-2/); [European PROGRES study](http://www.europeanprogres.org/dokumenti/48_908148_studija-srb-new.pdf) (via search snippets)
- The Kosovo ban on Serbian goods pushed Kosovo traders to more distant suppliers at higher prices. — [Kosovo Online](https://www.kosovo-online.com/en/news/politics/sahini-due-ban-imports-serbia-greatest-harm-serbian-citizens-and-albanians-were-also)
- The local economic agenda (Business Union "Vision" conference) focuses on agricultural product certification and access to markets, which suggests weak distribution and logistics for local producers. — [Gazeta Tema context](https://english.gazetatema.net/kosovo-region/albanian-mp-in-serbia-seeks-10m-for-preevo-valley-i341504) (via search snippet)

### Inferences
- If valley shops currently drive to Niš (about 150 km) or Skopje for supplies, a local stockist with Albanian-language service, Viber/WhatsApp ordering and delivery across Preševo, Bujanovac and Medveđa could cut their trips. This is a hypothesis to test by asking 10–20 salon, café and phone-shop owners where they buy, how often, and what they run out of.
- Demand would be strongly seasonal, peaking with diaspora visits and weddings in July and August and around Christmas, New Year and the Bajrams. Stocking for the December–January diaspora window is the nearest opportunity after a November 2026 start.

### Gaps
- No interviews, surveys or articles were found documenting supplier gaps in Preševo for salon supplies, packaging, phone accessories or auto parts.
- No data on e-commerce or courier use (for example, Serbian online shops delivering to Preševo, or Kosovo/North Macedonia social-media sellers), which would compete with any local trader.
