# Legal, Tax, Fiscalization, Inspection and Customs Rules for a Small Trading/Reselling Business in Preševo, Serbia (2025–2026)

Research date: 27 Oct 2026. Several primary sites (paragraf.rs, pausalko.rs, biznis.rs) could not be fetched directly (DNS errors in the research environment), so a lot of findings below come from search-result summaries of those pages. Treat amounts as indicative. Verify them against Službeni glasnik / purs.gov.rs / carina.rs before acting.

## 1. Registration as preduzetnik (APR), paušal eligibility for trade, monthly amounts in Preševo, bookkeeping alternative

### Takeaway
You can register as a preduzetnik only electronically via the APR portal, which needs a qualified e-certificate and a payment card. APR fees went up from 1 Jan 2026. The key finding: **retail and wholesale trade (NACE sector G, 45–47) are excluded from paušal (flat-rate) taxation**. The only exception is trade run from a kiosk, trailer or similar temporary/mobile structure. In practice a reseller (shop, online or B2B) must keep books ("knjigaš", taxed on real profit), or run on the kiosk exception.

### Cited Findings
- Electronic registration of entrepreneurs has been mandatory since 2023, not optional. It requires an APR portal account, a payment card and a qualified electronic certificate (source updated March 2026) — [search summary of zuniclaw.com / n1info](https://n1info.rs/biznis/apr-obavezna-e-registracija-visestruko-uvecala-broj-firmi-osnovanih-elektronski/); see also [n1info – APR e-registration service](https://n1info.rs/biznis/apr-pustio-u-rad-novi-servis-za-elektronsku-registraciju-osnivanja-firmi/)
- APR adopted a new fee decision with "viši iznosi od 1. januara 2026". The headline cites 8,000 RSD for founding a company (DOO) and 4,000 RSD for changing data. The preduzetnik fee is not confirmed in the snippet — [biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/apr-ima-nove-cene-registracija-osnivanje-firme-8-000-dinara-promena-podataka-4-000-dinara/)
- APR registration notices are now delivered only through e-Sanduče (e-Inbox) — [biznis.rs](https://biznis.rs/vesti/apr-paketi-za-pristup-podacima-uz-naknade-do-90-000-dinara/)
- Activities excluded from paušal include advertising/market research, **retail and wholesale trade**, hotels and restaurants, financial intermediation and real estate — [platnilistic.rs](https://www.platnilistic.rs/blog/pausalno-oporezivanje); [pausalko.rs – šifre delatnosti 2026](https://pausalko.rs/blog/sifre-delatnosti-pausalci-ko-moze-da-bude-pausalac/)
- Article 40 of the Zakon o porezu na dohodak građana (ZPDG): a trade or catering business run from a **kiosk, trailer or similar temporary/mobile structure** may, on request, be taxed on a flat-rate basis. This is the exception to the trade ban — [beoecc.rs – članovi 40–42 ZPDG](https://www.beoecc.rs/clanovi-40-42-zakona-o-porezu-na-dohodak-gradana/) (the text shown reflects amendments up to 5/2020, so check the current wording)
- Paušal turnover limit: below 6,000,000 RSD per calendar year. The separate VAT threshold is 8,000,000 RSD over 12 months — [platnilistic.rs](https://www.platnilistic.rs/blog/pausalno-oporezivanje); [cicapravnica.rs](https://cicapravnica.rs/pravni-saveti/pausalno-oporezivanje-preduzetnika)
- The deadline to request paušal (or a change of taxation method) for 2027 is **2 Nov 2026**. A newly registered entrepreneur applies at registration — [hitradio.rs](https://hitradio.rs/preduzetnici-imaju-rok-do-2-novembra-za-prijavu-pausalnog-oporezivanja); [insajder.net (2026 round)](https://insajder.net/prenosimo/poreska-uprava-zahtevi-za-pausalno-oporezivanje-za-2026-se-podnose-do-31-oktobra)
- The monthly paušal amount is fixed by a Tax Administration decision (rešenje). It depends on activity code, municipality and the average wage. The official estimator is informator.preduzetnistvo.gov.rs/kalkulator — [cicapravnica.rs](https://cicapravnica.rs/pravni-saveti/pausalno-oporezivanje-preduzetnika); [pausalko.rs calculator](https://pausalko.rs/alati/kalkulator-pausalnog-poreza/)
- National rough range for 2026: about 30,000–45,000 RSD/month in tax plus contributions. This is not Preševo-specific — [platnilistic.rs](https://www.platnilistic.rs/blog/koliko-pausalac-placa-mesecno)
- Contribution rates cited: PIO 24% (one source says 25.5%), health 10.3%, unemployment 0.75%, plus 10% income tax, for about 45% in total. **The sources conflict on the PIO rate** — [platica.rs](https://platica.rs/pausal); [platnilistic.rs](https://www.platnilistic.rs/pausal)
- The 2026 "test samostalnosti" (independence test, 9 criteria) applies to paušalci who work for one main client. This matters mostly for service providers, not traders — [advokati-rs.com](https://advokati-rs.com/articles/pausalno-oporezivanje-2026-i-devet-kriterijuma-testa/)
- A paušalac who loses eligibility (for example by becoming a VAT payer) must keep books from that date — [beoecc.rs](https://www.beoecc.rs/clanovi-40-42-zakona-o-porezu-na-dohodak-gradana/)

### Inferences
- A €5,000 reselling business (retail + B2B) almost certainly **cannot be paušal**. The realistic options are:
  - **(a) Preduzetnik knjigaš** (bookkeeping entrepreneur): 10% tax on actual profit, plus contributions on at least the minimum base. Expect to pay an accountant.
  - **(b) Kiosk-based trade** under the Art. 40 exception. This is narrow and needs a physical kiosk.
  - **(c) A DOO**: more fees and bookkeeping, but limited liability.
- Since Preševo is one of the least-developed municipalities, its paušal coefficients (where paušal applies) are likely in a lower group. This is an inference, not verified for 2026.
- Pairing a paušal-eligible service code (for example beauty/hair services) with *incidental* product sales is a grey area. Ask an accountant before relying on it.

### Gaps
- The exact 2026 APR fee for a preduzetnik: it was 1,500 RSD before 2026 (training knowledge, not verified here), and the 2026 increase was not confirmed for preduzetnici.
- Registration timeline: APR's legal deadline was historically 5 working days, often faster electronically. Not verified for 2026.
- Preševo-specific 2026 paušal amount and municipality group: not found. Use the official calculator.
- Minimum monthly contributions for a knjigaš in 2026 (on the minimum contribution base): not found in this research.
- The full current text of ZPDG Art. 40, and whether "internet trade" (47.91) is treated differently: not confirmed.

## 2. E-fiskalizacija (fiscal device/software) and e-invoicing (SEF) for B2B

### Takeaway
Every sale to an individual (promet na malo) must have a fiscal receipt issued through an ESIR plus a processor (V-PFR or L-PFR), whether you are a paušalac or a knjigaš. The Tax Administration supplies the security element for free, and its Android "ESIR Poreska uprava RS" app is also free. Sales only to companies need no fiscal receipt, but they fall under e-invoicing (SEF). The SEF obligations for non-VAT entrepreneurs are not fully clear from 2026 sources.

### Cited Findings
- Every entrepreneur who sells to individuals must issue fiscal receipts. Paušal status does not exempt you. Exemptions depend on activity (Uredba list), not on how you are taxed — [pausalko.rs – fiskalna kasa za paušalce 2026](https://pausalko.rs/blog/da-li-pausalci-moraju-da-imaju-fiskalnu-kasu-i-ko-je-oslobodjen-obaveze-evidentiranja-prometa-preko-kase/); [ralink.rs](https://ralink.rs/fiskalizacija/fiskalna-kasa-za-pausalce-kada-je-obavezna-a-kada-nije/)
- If you invoice only legal entities or entrepreneurs, you issue no fiscal receipts, because that is not retail. A shop that mainly sells to individuals must fiscalize every sale on its premises, including sales to firms, and needs at least one L-PFR on site — [pausalko.rs](https://pausalko.rs/blog/da-li-pausalci-moraju-da-imaju-fiskalnu-kasu-i-ko-je-oslobodjen-obaveze-evidentiranja-prometa-preko-kase/)
- Market-stall (pijačna tezga) sellers are exempt until **31 Dec 2026**. This deadline has been extended repeatedly — [pausalko.rs](https://pausalko.rs/blog/da-li-pausalci-moraju-da-imaju-fiskalnu-kasu-i-ko-je-oslobodjen-obaveze-evidentiranja-prometa-preko-kase/)
- Exempt activities include transport (city/taxi), air transport, post, telecom, finance/insurance, legal services and others. **Trade, hairdressers and beauty salons are not exempt** — [feruvi.rs fiscal guide 2026](https://feruvi.rs/blog/fiskalne-kase); [pausalko.rs](https://pausalko.rs/blog/da-li-pausalci-moraju-da-imaju-fiskalnu-kasu-i-ko-je-oslobodjen-obaveze-evidentiranja-prometa-preko-kase/)
- The security element (bezbednosni element) is issued free by the Tax Administration. The ESIR and processor come from commercial suppliers at varying prices. The free Android "ESIR Poreska uprava RS" app works with V-PFR and L-PFR, but it is Android-only and has no KPO book or limit tracking — [epreduzetnik.rs](https://epreduzetnik.rs/esir-aplikacija-poreske-uprave/); [pausalko.rs](https://pausalko.rs/blog/online-fiskalna-kasa-za-pausalce/)
- Commercial online cash registers ("eKasa", no device needed) exist as cheap subscription options — [pausalko.rs/fiskalna-kasa](https://pausalko.rs/fiskalna-kasa/); [intellipos.rs](https://www.intellipos.rs/fiskalna-kasa-za-pausalce.html)
- Fines for fiscalization breaches for an entrepreneur: 50,000–500,000 RSD — [pausalko.rs](https://pausalko.rs/blog/da-li-pausalci-moraju-da-imaju-fiskalnu-kasu-i-ko-je-oslobodjen-obaveze-evidentiranja-prometa-preko-kase/)
- SEF B2B e-invoicing has been mandatory since 1 Jan 2023 for VAT payers. Non-VAT entrepreneurs (mostly paušalci) face many open questions in dealing with SEF users — [biznis.rs (2023)](https://biznis.rs/vesti/srbija/kako-e-fakture-mogu-slati-i-evidentirati-pausalci/); [vatcalc](https://www.vatcalc.com/serbia/serbia-b2b-e-invoicing-jan-2023/)
- The Ministry of Finance postponed further SEF reforms (extra fields, e-archiving, stricter VAT corrections) to the end of 2026. Other sources say 2027 — **the sources conflict** — [fiscal-requirements.com](https://www.fiscal-requirements.com/news/4560-serbia-has-postponed-the-sef-b2b-e-invoicing-reforms-until-the-end-of-2026); [invoicedataextraction.com](https://invoicedataextraction.com/blog/serbia-e-invoicing-requirements)
- For some retail transactions in tax periods after 31 Mar 2026, an e-invoice is also required after the fiscal receipt — [invoicedataextraction.com](https://invoicedataextraction.com/blog/serbia-e-invoicing-requirements) (a secondary source, not verified)
- Electronic delivery notes (e-otpremnice) were introduced in 2026. Whether they apply to paušalci depends on the case — [pausalko.rs](https://pausalko.rs/blog/elektronske-otpremnice-2026-da-li-se-ticu-pausalaca/)

### Inferences
- The cheapest compliant setup for occasional cash/card sales to individuals is an Android phone running the free PU ESIR app with a V-PFR, plus the free security element. Commercial online registers are the next step up.
- B2B sales to VAT-registered buyers will in practice run through SEF. A non-VAT seller can usually register in SEF and send invoices there, which many large buyers expect.

### Gaps
- Exact current prices of the cheapest commercial ESIR/L-PFR packages: not found (suppliers' price lists are needed).
- The exact 2026 SEF obligations of non-VAT preduzetnici (mandatory vs voluntary issuing): not confirmed by a primary source. Check the MoF efaktura.gov.rs FAQ.

## 3. Selling online/Instagram and from home: rules, penalties for unregistered trading, goods without documentation, seizure

### Takeaway
Selling through Instagram, Facebook or from home without registration counts as illegal trade if it is continuous and organized. Market inspectors actively track social-media sellers (including through postal records) and seize goods. Fines cited for individuals run roughly 50,000–150,000 RSD under the Trade Law (unverified secondary source). A separate range of 50,000–500,000 RSD applies under the Inspection Oversight Law for unregistered activity. Large-scale cases can bring criminal liability (Criminal Code Art. 235).

### Cited Findings
- The Zakon o trgovini gives market inspectors powers including temporary bans on trading certain goods, temporary closure of premises and bans on certain sales forms — [paragraf.rs – Zakon o trgovini](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/zakon_o_trgovini.html); [must.gov.rs](https://must.gov.rs/tekst/sr/22375/zakon-o-trgovini.php)
- An individual trading without registration faces a fine of **50,000–150,000 RSD**, plus a possible ban on the activity for 6 months to 2 years. Goods are seized, and seizure is permanent for repeat offences. The source cites a 2026 amendment (Sl. glasnik 35/2026), which is **not verified** — [rxnwebsolutions.com (blog)](https://rxnwebsolutions.com/prodaja-preko-instagrama-zakon-porez/)
- Under the Zakon o inspekcijskom nadzoru, an individual running an activity as an unregistered entity faces 50,000–500,000 RSD. This is reported in the context of labour/inspection oversight — [RTV](https://www.rtv.rs/sr_lat/vojvodina/novi-sad/visoke-kazne-za-neregistrovane-preduzetnike_687849.html); [aktivasistem.com](https://aktivasistem.com/news/inspekcija-rada-kontrolise-neregistrovane-firme/)
- Inspectors work with the postal inspection to identify senders of goods sold online. If an illegal trader is caught, "roba se pleni" (goods are seized). Inspectors check whether pages show a PIB and business details — [Telegraf](https://www.telegraf.rs/vesti/ekonomija/3026293-inspektori-jure-nelegalne-trgovce-na-fejsbuku-i-instagramu-a-onda-im-upadaju-u-kucu-na-udaru-su-majstori-i-prodavci-stvari-koje-najcesce-narucujemo); [Telegraf](https://www.telegraf.rs/vesti/ekonomija/2985548-instagram-i-fejsbuk-trgovci-debelo-zagazili-u-mutne-radnje-ako-prodajete-robu-na-ovaj-nacin-mogu-debelo-da-vas-kazne)
- E-commerce law amendments: inspectors can order removal of illegal listings on Facebook and Instagram within two days. The fines cited are 1.5 million RSD for companies and 300,000 RSD for entrepreneurs (as reported when proposed). The most-sold "black" online goods are tobacco, medicines, clothing, footwear and toys — [paragraf.rs news](https://www.paragraf.rs/dnevne-vesti/240719/240719-vest2.html); [paragraf.rs news 2022](https://www.paragraf.rs/dnevne-vesti/011222/011222-vest7.html)
- The new consumer-protection law widens the definition of "trader" to cover individuals who de facto trade. Inspectors also check individuals on KupujemProdajem, Ananas and Limundo — [mojnovisad.com](https://www.mojnovisad.com/novi-zakoni-menjaju-onlajn-prodaju-inspekcija-proverava-i-gradane-na-kp-ananasu-limundu/)
- Criminal Code Art. 235 (unauthorized trade): fine or up to 2 years' imprisonment, more if the gain exceeds 450,000 RSD — [rxnwebsolutions.com](https://rxnwebsolutions.com/prodaja-preko-instagrama-zakon-porez/) (secondary source, verify)
- An older employers' guide gives market-inspection misdemeanour fines of only 5,000–50,000 RSD. **This is outdated** — [poslodavci.rs](https://poslodavci.rs/trzisna-inspekcija/trzisna_inspekcija_-_vodic_za_poslodavce/)

### Inferences
- In Preševo, cross-border goods with no import documents (no JCI/invoice) are at particular risk. An inspector can seize them even from a registered business that cannot show their origin.
- Even after registering, an online shop/Instagram seller must display business details (name, PIB, address) and follow distance-selling consumer rules (for example the 14-day withdrawal right). This was not verified in this research.

### Gaps
- The official current text of the Zakon o trgovini penal provisions and seizure article could not be fetched (paragraf.rs unreachable). Exact 2025–2026 amounts are unconfirmed.
- Whether the e-commerce law amendment with the takedown fines was enacted, and when.

## 4. Any legal way to resell without registration?

### Takeaway
Only occasional sales of your own used personal items are tolerated. Buying goods in order to resell them continuously and in an organized way is trade, and it requires registration. There is no turnover-based "small seller" exemption for reselling in Serbia.

### Cited Findings
- An individual who periodically sells used personal items does not become a fiscal obligor. An individual who sells goods continuously and in an organized way to earn income must register — [mojnovisad.com](https://www.mojnovisad.com/novi-zakoni-menjaju-onlajn-prodaju-inspekcija-proverava-i-gradane-na-kp-ananasu-limundu/)
- Inspectors look at whether the activity is de facto trade, regardless of registration — [mojnovisad.com](https://www.mojnovisad.com/novi-zakoni-menjaju-onlajn-prodaju-inspekcija-proverava-i-gradane-na-kp-ananasu-limundu/); [rxnwebsolutions.com](https://rxnwebsolutions.com/prodaja-preko-instagrama-zakon-porez/)
- Market-stall sellers are exempt from fiscalization until 31 Dec 2026, but they still need to be registered — [pausalko.rs](https://pausalko.rs/blog/da-li-pausalci-moraju-da-imaju-fiskalnu-kasu-i-ko-je-oslobodjen-obaveze-evidentiranja-prometa-preko-kase/)

### Inferences
- Legal low-cost paths: (a) register as a preduzetnik (knjigaš) and keep costs minimal; (b) sell as a commission agent (sales on behalf of a registered firm under a contract); (c) sell your own home-made products as a registered entrepreneur. Option (b) is not verified as an employment or tax structure here.
- Agricultural producers (registered farms) have a separate regime for selling their own products, which is not relevant to resale.

### Gaps
- No primary source defines "occasional" in numbers (count or value). The judgment is case by case.

## 5. Customs: importing goods for resale from North Macedonia (CEFTA), Kosovo, Turkey; allowances; EORI; Preševo–Tabanovce

### Takeaway
Goods for resale are commercial imports. They do not qualify for the traveller's personal allowance, which is €100 per person. Commercial goods need a customs declaration (JCI) filed through a customs agent (špediter) under the importer's PIB, so in practice you need a registered business. The VAT base is the customs value (CIF) plus duty, at 20% (10% for some goods). Goods of preferential origin from North Macedonia under CEFTA (proved by EUR.1 or an invoice declaration) and from Turkey under the Serbia–Turkey FTA can enter at reduced or 0% duty, but VAT is always due. The Preševo–Tabanovce crossing is the main road crossing with North Macedonia.

### Cited Findings
- The traveller allowance is goods for personal use worth up to **€100** per person, free of duty and VAT. Allowances cannot be combined between travellers, and the allowance is for personal use only. For €100–€3,000 a simplified border procedure applies, using a 10% flat duty plus 20% VAT. This does not cover goods for resale — [biznis.rs – pravila uvoza 2025](https://biznis.rs/preduzetnik/porezi/sta-se-sve-moze-uneti-u-srbiju-bez-carine/); [kurir.rs](https://www.kurir.rs/vesti/drustvo/3830223/carina-srbije-carinjenje-cigarete-pice-parfemi)
- Rules on customs-approved treatment of goods — [carina.rs – Uredba o carinski dozvoljenom postupanju s robom](https://www.carina.rs/upload/media/2021/11/5/51998/Uredba_o_carinski_dozvoljenom_postupanju_s_robom-lat.pdf)
- Goods from North Macedonia need a CMR, invoice, packing list and, for preferential origin, an EUR.1 certificate or an invoice origin declaration (CEFTA). Customs clearance happens at the Preševo/Tabanovce crossing, and a JCI (single customs document) is required. Agri-food goods use CEFTA TRACES e-certificates (from March 2024) — [kombiprevozmaratonci.rs](https://kombiprevozmaratonci.rs/blog/carinske-regulative-srbija-makedonija-2026); [PKS – Poslovanje Severna Makedonija](https://api.pks.rs/storage/assets/PF%20Srbija%20-%20Severna%20Makedonija.pdf); [milnik016.com](https://milnik016.com/transport-srbija-makedonija)
- The duty base is the CIF value (goods, transport and insurance to the border) at the tariff-line rate. The VAT base is the customs value plus duty, usually at 20%. A common mistake is to compute VAT on the goods price alone. Domestic transport after clearance is not in the base — [fakturko.io](https://fakturko.io/blog/uvoz-robe-kako-izracunati-pdv-i-carinu); [fakturko.io – PDV pri uvozu](https://fakturko.io/blog/obracun-pdv-a-pri-uvozu-robe-osnovica-i-odbitak)
- Preferential rates depend on tariff line and origin agreement. See the Uredba o carinskim povlasticama — [paragraf.rs](https://www.paragraf.rs/propisi/uredba-o-carinskim-povlasticama.html); [tenasped.com](https://tenasped.com/blog/carinske-procedure-uvoz-robe)
- Kosovo: in Nov 2018 Kosovo imposed 100% taxes on goods from Serbia and BiH, later lifted. Trade has not recovered. Current 2026 rules for goods moving from Kosovo into Serbia were not found — [RSE / slobodnaevropa.org](https://www.slobodnaevropa.org/a/kosovo-izvoz-srbija-trgovina/31706854.html)
- 2026 customs cost calculator (unofficial) — [racunajmo.com](https://racunajmo.com/srbija/carina/)

### Inferences
- With €5,000, VAT of 20% plus duty plus agent fees can easily absorb 25–35% of goods value on non-preferential imports. A non-VAT entrepreneur cannot recover the import VAT, so it is a real cost.
- Goods from Kosovo cross administrative crossings (for example Končulj/Merdare). Serbia does not treat them as a customs border in the usual sense, but documentation and origin issues are complex and politically sensitive, so check with Uprava carina.
- Turkish goods (textiles, cosmetics, electronics) shipped via North Macedonia keep Turkish origin. Whether preferential treatment applies depends on proof of origin and the cumulation rules.

### Gaps
- EORI: Serbia's customs code (2019 law) introduced an EORI number for economic operators, but this research did not confirm the procedure. Importers generally act through their PIB plus a customs agent.
- Exact current status of Kosovo–Serbia goods flows in 2026: not found.
- Specific tariff rates for target goods (phone chargers, cosmetics, clothing) under CEFTA and the Serbia–Turkey FTA: not looked up.
- Typical špediter fees at Preševo/Tabanovce: not found.

## 6. Product rules: cosmetics, electrical safety (chargers), counterfeit brands

### Takeaway
Cosmetics placed on the Serbian market need a Serbian responsible person, who for imports is the importer (a registered legal entity). Sanitary inspection can sample goods at import, at the importer's cost. EU CPNP notification is not required for Serbia. For chargers and counterfeit goods this research found no primary sources. Known rules (Serbian "low-voltage" technical regulation with conformity marking; Law on Trademarks, Criminal Code; customs IP border measures) are listed as gaps.

### Cited Findings
- The importer of cosmetics is the Serbia-based legal entity bringing the product in. It may appoint a Serbia-based responsible person in writing. CPNP (EU) notification is not a condition for the Serbian market. ALIMS can give an expert opinion on product classification via its e-portal — [PKS – Vodič za stavljanje kozmetičkog proizvoda na tržište 2022](https://api.pks.rs/storage/assets/Vodi%C4%8D%20za%20stavljanje%20kozmeti%C4%8Dkog%20proizvoda%20na%20tr%C5%BEi%C5%A1te.pdf)
- At import, the sanitary inspector may sample cosmetics for lab analysis at the importer's cost. A health certificate and conformity certificate are typically submitted — [spedicija-beograd.com](https://spedicija-beograd.com/blog/uvoz-kozmetike-u-srbiju/)
- From 1 Jan 2025, products not compliant with EU Reg. 2022/2195 may not be placed on the RS market. Serbia had not yet aligned with Reg. 2024/996 — [PKS June 2025](https://api.pks.rs/storage/assets/A%C5%BEuriranje%20kozmeti%C4%8Dke%20dokumentacije%20FINAL,%20PKS,%2016.6.2025..pdf)

### Inferences
- Buying salon cosmetics from a Serbian-registered distributor (with a Serbian label and declaration) moves the importer obligations to the distributor. This is much simpler than importing them yourself.
- Chargers and other electricals bought cheaply abroad without conformity marking and documentation are high-risk for seizure by market inspection.

### Gaps
- The charger conformity regime (Serbian low-voltage and EMC technical regulations, conformity marking): not verified in this research.
- Penalties for selling counterfeit brand goods (Zakon o žigovima, Criminal Code art. 233 on trademark infringement, customs IP measures): not verified.
- Cosmetics labelling in Serbian: required in practice, but not cited here.

## 7. Incentives: NSZ self-employment subsidy, Coordination Body (Preševo/Bujanovac/Medveđa) grants

### Takeaway
The NSZ self-employment subsidy for 2025–2026 is **380,000 RSD** (420,000 RSD for persons with disabilities). It is paid as a lump sum to registered unemployed persons who complete NSZ business training and a business plan. Calls are local and per branch, and the 2026 rounds have largely closed. The Coordination Body for Preševo, Bujanovac and Medveđa ran a 2026 call (24.4M RSD, up to 70% co-financing of equipment for micro/small firms and entrepreneurs). It closed on 26 Jun 2026 with 9 contracts awarded. A new founder would need to wait for a 2027 call, and that call targets existing businesses buying equipment.

### Inferences
- Applicant numbers are low (14 across three municipalities), so the odds may be good for an eligible applicant in the 2027 round. However, the call requires an existing registered business and own co-funding of 30% or more, and it targets equipment, not trade stock.
- The NSZ subsidy (about €3,250 at roughly 117 RSD/€) could nearly double a €5,000 budget. To qualify, the person must be registered as unemployed at the NSZ Preševo/Vranje branch *before* registering the business, and typically must keep the business active for at least 12 months. The 12-month condition is not verified for 2026.
